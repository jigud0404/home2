FAQ

Game Dubbing FAQ

How is the cost of game dubbing calculated?

Pricing depends on the number of characters, script volume, number of voice actors, editing scope, and delivery requirements. If you send us a script or estimated volume, we can provide a more accurate estimate.

Can we contact you before the script is finalized?

Yes. We can start by discussing the number of characters, estimated gameplay volume, and types of voice lines needed to outline the production scope and schedule.

Can you handle voice casting?

Yes. Based on your character profiles and desired tone, we can suggest voice candidates and support sample review or auditions when needed.

Can we attend recording sessions online?

Yes. We support online recording attendance via ZOOM, allowing you to communicate with the voice talent and provide real-time feedback during the session.
